# Pure Glow Care — Visitor Guide

Welcome to **Pure Glow Care**, your online destination for premium hair care. This guide explains how to use the public website as a customer or visitor.

## Browsing the site

### Home

The landing page introduces the brand with:

- Hero message and buttons to **Shop Collection** or read **Our Story**
- Why Pure Glow — natural ingredients, nourishment, safety, results
- Featured products (when configured)
- Customer photos (**real results**) and written reviews
- Call-to-action to explore the full shop

### Products

Open **Products** in the menu to see the full catalog. Each card shows:

- Product image (or placeholder if none yet)
- Name, short summary, and **price in UGX**
- Badge labels such as “Featured” or “Sold out”

Tap a product for the detail page with:

- Full description, purpose, how to use, and ingredients
- Optional product video
- Stock status

### Testimonials

The **Testimonials** page has two sections when content exists:

1. **Glow Gallery** — photos and videos from real customers
2. **Written reviews** — star ratings and quotes with author names

### Our Story

Learn about the brand mission, values, and link back to the product collection.

### Contact

Use the footer on any page for:

- Phone and email
- WhatsApp link
- Instagram

Details are managed by the store owner and update when they change settings in admin.

## Mobile and tablet

The site is responsive: navigation collapses to a menu icon on smaller screens. Gallery grids adjust from multiple columns to one column on phones.

## Ordering products

The current site is a **showcase catalog**. Purchasing may be arranged offline via WhatsApp, phone, or email using the footer contacts unless a checkout feature is added later.

## Privacy

- The public site does not require an account to browse.
- Only site administrators can sign in at `/admin`.

## Getting help

For product questions, orders, or delivery, contact Pure Glow Care using the channels in the footer. For website issues, contact your store administrator.
